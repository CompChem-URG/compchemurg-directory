# CompChemURG Directory

A community-maintained directory of people from underrepresented groups working in and around computational chemistry — connecting researchers, making underrepresented voices in the field more visible, and helping people find one another.

This project is part of [CompChemURG](https://compchem-urg.github.io/compchemurg/), and was inspired by USC's [Women in Theoretical/Computational Chemistry](https://iopenshell.usc.edu/wtc/) directory. It's an independent, unaffiliated project — every entry here is added by the person themselves, not copied from elsewhere.

Browse the [Directory](/directory/), or [add your own entry](/contribute/).
