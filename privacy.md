# Privacy

Everything in this directory was submitted by the person it describes, and anyone listed can have their entry removed by asking. Questions: [compchemurg@gmail.com](mailto:compchemurg@gmail.com).

**Who runs it.** The CompChemURG maintainers, as a volunteer project. Nothing here is commercial.

**What's published.** Whatever you chose to give us: name, affiliation, links, research areas, and optionally location, bio, photo, and group(s) you self-identify with. All of it is public, on this site and in the [repository](https://github.com/CompChem-URG/compchemurg-directory).

**Consent.** We publish your entry because you asked us to, and you can change your mind at any time. Entries are added either by the person themselves or by a maintainer transcribing what that person sent us — never on someone else's say-so. If you find an entry about you that you didn't ask for, tell us and it goes immediately. The self-identification field can reveal sensitive information such as ethnicity or sexual orientation, so it is optional, never inferred, and empty unless you fill it in yourself.

**Email.** If you email us we keep it, both to reply and as the record of your consent. We don't publish it or use it for anything else.

**Removal.** Ask by [removal request](https://github.com/CompChem-URG/compchemurg-directory/issues/new?template=removal-request.yml) or [email](mailto:compchemurg@gmail.com). Anyone can ask, not only the person listed. What that reaches:

- **This site** — gone within minutes.
- **Repository history** — stays in past commits unless we rewrite the history. We are happy to do that on request.
- **Copies others already made** — forks, clones and search-engine caches of this project are outside our reach.

**Your rights.** If you want to see, correct, or delete what we hold, or withdraw consent at any time (which takes your entry down) then please email us.
